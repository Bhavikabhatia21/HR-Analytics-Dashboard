# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary trends, and departmental performance. The dashboard provides HR managers and business leaders with actionable insights to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes HR data to identify employee attrition patterns and workforce trends through interactive visualizations and KPI metrics.

The dashboard enables users to filter data by multiple dimensions and quickly understand key HR metrics.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition
- Monitor workforce demographics
- Compare departments based on salary and attrition
- Identify high-risk employee groups
- Build an interactive HR dashboard for decision making

## 💡 Key Insights

- Overall employee attrition rate is **16.12%**
- Research & Development has the highest attrition.
- Sales department has the highest average monthly income.
- Most employees belong to Sales and Research & Development.
- Attrition is higher during the initial years of employment.
